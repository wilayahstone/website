# Business inputs for the manual-order store

Updated 10 November 2026. Online gateways are removed; no gateway approval or card API credentials are needed for this operating model.

## Provided and remaining business inputs

- Primary WhatsApp contact is now supplied and active: +92 370 5198895. Public support email links have been removed.
- Legal seller name, business address and support contact. Save these in protected Settings; each new order preserves that information.
- Actual product photographs, measured silver weight/tolerance, stone dimensions, identity, origin and treatment disclosures, ring-size system and approved engraving proofs.
- Approved retail prices, actual costs, justified margins and destination-specific shipping charges. A blank template value is not a zero cost or a selling price.
- Supplier/maker identity, reservation terms, real available allocation, capacity, production lead times and quality responsibilities.
- Actual receiving-account or wallet instructions to share directly with the customer after agreement. Never request banking passwords, card details or one-time codes. Only record a receipt after checking received funds.
- Courier acceptance, route and charges for each destination, including supplier-to-Karachi and Karachi-to-customer handling. Record the carrier and exact delivery commitment in the agreement.
- Named design/content reviewers and refund decision makers. Religious review remains a qualified human responsibility.
- Real foreign exchange rates for private PKR reports, with their update date. Customer display conversions remain estimates and do not amend an agreed order currency.
- Approved domain and DNS changes if the company wishes to connect a custom domain. No ownership or subscription transfer is implied by website publication.

## Operating sequence

Customer enquiry → exact written specification and full quote → recorded agreement → actual full receipt → supplier production → quality check → Karachi receiving → agreed customer dispatch → delivery → after-sales and any manager-approved repayment.

The portal supplies the records and safeguards. It does not invent commercial facts, automatically send WhatsApp messages, transfer funds or certify a transaction’s religious compliance.
